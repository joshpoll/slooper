Slooper

Slow it down, loop it, count yourself in.

  1. Open a YouTube link above, or tap Load clip for a video or audio file (a screen recording works).
  2. Drag the red A and B markers, set a speed, and press Play.
  3. Clips get a waveform and automatic beats. YouTube videos get them too if the song has been analyzed; otherwise tap Tap beat along with the music to lay down the eight-count.

Loop points, speed and beat grid are remembered for each video.
